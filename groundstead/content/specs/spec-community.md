purpose: Help a reader build a small local support circle and know whom to call in an emergency.
audience:
  - staying-in-city
  - decided-to-move
  - organization
required_sections:
  - id: neighbours
    heading: Know your neighbours
    must_cover:
      - Share contact lists, find out who needs help and list skills.
  - id: skill-exchange
    heading: Skill exchange
    must_cover:
      - Simple ways to trade skills and tools, and safe ways to meet.
  - id: local-contacts
    heading: Local contacts
    must_cover:
      - The local emergency number, disaster agency, Red Cross or Red Crescent society and community leaders.
  - id: support-circle
    heading: A circle of ten households
    must_cover:
      - Roles and a regular check-in routine.
  - id: safeguarding
    heading: Privacy and care
    must_cover:
      - Protect privacy, and take particular care with children and older people.
safety_points:
  - Do not publish a household's address or daily schedule.
  - Check on older and disabled neighbours before and during emergencies.
  - Official instructions come first.
red_lines:
  - Do not include weapons, militia, vigilante or surveillance content.
  - Do not encourage confronting looters or strangers.
  - Do not encourage sharing unverified rumours.
  - Do not say that a place, route or building is safe or open.
  - Do not promise or guarantee any outcome.
  - Do not include weapons or instructions to evade the law.

  count: 1

  - local_examples
