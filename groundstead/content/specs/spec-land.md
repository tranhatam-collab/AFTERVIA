  - id: climate-and-hazards
    heading: Climate and hazards
    must_cover:

      - How to check the official hazard maps and visit in the wet season.
  - id: water-and-soil
    heading: Water and soil
    must_cover:
      - The water source and who has rights to it.
      - Soil and drainage; never claim a test result.
  - id: access-and-care
    heading: Access and care
    must_cover:
      - Distance to a clinic and a hospital.
      - Road access in the wet season and mobile coverage.
  - id: legal-questions
    heading: Questions for a local lawyer
    must_cover:
      - Who owns it, what the land may be used for, permits, transfer rules and any rules for foreigners.
      - State the questions only, never the answers.
  - id: scoring-sheet
    heading: Scoring sheet
    must_cover:
      - A sheet the reader fills in to compare plots.

  - Check official flood and landslide maps and visit in the wet season.
  - Plan how an injured person would reach care.

  - Do not give legal advice or say that a plot is legal or safe to buy.
  - Do not rank listings, brokers or agents.
  - Do not say that a place, route or building is safe or open.
  - Do not promise or guarantee any outcome.
  - Do not include weapons or instructions to evade the law.

  - Every article carries an "ask a local lawyer" box.
