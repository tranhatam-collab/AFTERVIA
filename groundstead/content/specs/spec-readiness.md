layer: SPEC
id: spec-readiness
locale: en
version: 1
module: readiness
updated: 2026-11-06
purpose: Let a reader measure how ready they are across the seven pillars and name a next step, without pushing them to leave the city.
audience:
  - urban-hesitant
  - decided-to-move
required_sections:
  - id: how-it-works
    heading: How the 12-question check works
    must_cover:
      - It takes about five minutes and runs on the reader's device.
      - Nothing is sent anywhere unless the reader chooses to save.
      - Scores are shown per pillar, never as one pass-or-fail grade.
  - id: seven-pillars
    heading: What each pillar measures
    must_cover:
      - Water, energy, food, shelter and sanitation, health, money and community, one plain sentence each.
      - What a low score means in practice.
  - id: honest-risks
    heading: What usually goes wrong
    must_cover:
      - Money running out in the first year.
      - Health care being far away.
      - Isolation, and skills that take time to learn.
      - Say plainly that country life is not automatically safer than city life.
  - id: next-step
    heading: Your next step
    must_cover:
      - One concrete step for each low pillar, linked to the matching module.
      - The option to stay in the city and do the 72-hour mode first.
safety_points:
  - State that the score is a planning aid, not a medical, legal or financial assessment.
  - Tell readers with health conditions to plan access to care first.
red_lines:
  - Do not recommend leaving the city or buying land on the strength of a score.
  - Do not label a reader "ready" or "unprepared" as a verdict.
  - Do not say that a place, route or building is safe or open.
  - Do not promise or guarantee any outcome.
  - Do not include weapons or instructions to evade the law.
minimum_sources:
  count: 1
  note: Cite the sources behind each pillar's measure; the quiz wording itself is Groundstead's own.
local_must_have:
  - units
  - currency
  - local_examples
tools:
  - readiness-quiz
packs: []
disclaimer: none
